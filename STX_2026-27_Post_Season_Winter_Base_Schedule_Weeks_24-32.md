# ST. XAVIER 2026–27 POST-SEASON & WINTER BASE SCHEDULE
## All Groups — Weeks 24–32 (November 2, 2026 – January 3, 2027)

*The main table each week is the default schedule for athletes NOT competing in the post season. The "Post Season Overrides" table holds workouts for athletes still competing.*

**Mileage rules:** weekly mileage goes up no more than 10% during a build, and winter peak is capped at the lower of XC peak + 5 miles or XC peak + 10% (Blue 28, White 38.5, Green 49.5, Gold 60).

**How to fill in a week:** replace each `**Workout TBD**` using the same format as the XC file, for example `Easy *4mi*`, `**Long Run** *8mi*`, `**REST**`, and add the mileage to the header, for example `Gold (32 mi)`. In the override table, fill in only the days and groups that differ; blank cells follow the main table. A week needs no override table once the post season is over.

## **PACE GUIDE (ALL GROUPS)**

- **@E:** Easy pace (conversational, 65–75% max HR)
- **@T:** Threshold pace (comfortably hard, 15K–HM race effort, 85–90% max HR)
- **@I:** Interval pace (VO2 max, 3K–5K race effort, 95–100% max HR)
- **@R:** Repetition pace (speed work, mile pace or faster, neuromuscular power)
- **@RP:** Race pace (current 5K fitness level)
