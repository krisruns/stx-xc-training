⛰ Hill reps: 10m climb over the first 270m, then 100m downhill to the finish.

R effort on a hill: quick, light steps, strong arm drive, run tall, no overstriding. Don't sprint the bottom of the hill; the rep is about holding good form over the top. On the downhill, stay tall with quick feet under the hips. Let gravity help, but don't bomb it or heel-strike to brake.

Before: WU, Dynamics, stride progression. After: Strides, Mobility/Strength A.
